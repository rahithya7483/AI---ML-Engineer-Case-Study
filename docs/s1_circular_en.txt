GOVERNMENT OF EXAMPLE STATE
Department of Women and Child Development
Circular No. WCD/2026/114 dated 12 September 2026

Subject: Launch of Mahila Udyam Sahayata Yojana

1. The State Government has approved the Mahila Udyam Sahayata Yojana
to support women who wish to start small businesses.

2. Benefit: A one-time grant of Rs. 25,000 will be given to each
selected beneficiary.

[Page 2]
3. Eligibility: (a) Woman applicant aged 18 to 45 years.
(b) Annual family income below Rs. 2,00,000.
(c) Permanent resident of the State.

4. Documents required: Aadhaar card, income certificate,
bank passbook copy.

5. How to apply: Applications are to be submitted online through
the district industries office portal.

6. Last date of application: 31 March 2027.
